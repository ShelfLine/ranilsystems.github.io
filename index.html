Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our CCTV installation and maintenance service.

Can I view my security cameras live on my phone while away from home?
Yes! We configure free, encrypted mobile applications (Hik-Connect, gDMSS, CP PLUS, or Ezviz) on your smartphones and tablets so you can view live video and past playback from anywhere in the world.
Do the cameras record video in total darkness?
All our modern cameras come equipped with either Smart Infrared (black-and-white night vision up to 20m - 40m) or ColorVu / Full-Color technology which provides crystal clear full-color video even in pitch-black settings.
How many days of video recording can the system store?
Recording time depends on the hard drive capacity and number of cameras. A standard 1TB surveillance-grade drive typically holds 15 to 20 days of continuous recording for a 4-camera setup using H.265 compression, and even longer with motion-activated recording.
Do you provide warranty and after-sales service?
Yes, all hardware comes with official 1 to 1 Years manufacturer replacement warranty. Additionally, Ranil Systems provides comprehensive after-sales service and Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC).
